# Tsuru Ecosystem — Social Economy Alignment (Phase 9)

**Scope:** Reconciles the Social Solidarity Economy (SSE) principles claimed by the landing (`landing-client/src/content/*.json`) and the strategic research (`docs/Rebranding Web_ Economía Indígena Solidaria.txt` — Bribri-grounded principles) against the **actual platform mechanics** verified in Phases 2–7 (markets-api, POS, cross-app-be, jbiller, data-api, storefronts, landing).
**Method:** Principle-by-principle audit. "Supported today?" refers to *product mechanics in code*, not copy. Status vocabulary: **Mechanics** (embodied in working code) · **Partial** · **Marketing-only** (exists solely as landing copy) · **Absent** · **Contradicted** (implementation actively violates the principle).
**Inputs:** `tsuru_landing_audit.md`, `tsuru_capability_inventory.md`, `tsuru_system_discovery.md`, `tsuru_domain_model.md`, `tsuru_user_journeys.md`, `tsuru_architecture_audit.md`. Absence claims re-verified by grep (`trueque|barter|feria|fair_|cooperativ` → zero hits in `server/src`; `trueque|barter|exchange_offer|mutual` → zero hits in `cross-app-be/app`).

## 2. Which principles live in the mechanics (not just copy)

Only three principles have any real embodiment in code, and each is qualified:

1. **Local development (#9) — the platform's authentic SSE asset.** The mature pillar of the ecosystem is *exactly* the thing a Costa Rican micro-seller cannot get from Shopify: legally valid Hacienda v4.4 e-invoicing (jbiller pipeline), CR fiscal catalogs, SINPE, WhatsApp-first selling, and offline-first POS for unreliable connectivity. This is genuine "tecnología al servicio de las personas" — but it is framed nowhere in the landing, which instead markets the unbuilt barter/fairs layer. The strongest implemented capability and the strongest marketed capability are disjoint sets.
2. **Intra-org collaboration (#11).** Memberships, invitations, assignments, and cash-closing reconciliation are real multi-person mechanics — though degraded by broken journeys (cajero dead-end, unmounted ClosingFlow, lost invitation redirects) and unenforced roles.
3. **Free access (#8).** True today because no billing code exists at all — a fact, not a designed commitment.

**Conversely, two principles are actively contradicted by mechanics:** transparency (#7: fake contact form, simulated publish success, placeholder traction claims) and fairness-by-system-rules (#6: ecosystem-wide IDOR, unmounted RBAC, plaintext fiscal credentials). For a brand whose three trust pills are *Transparencia / Comercio justo / Comunidad local*, the implementation currently falsifies the first two.

## 3. Which principles are landing rhetoric only

The entire community/SSE differentiator layer: **barter (#1), fairs (#2), cross-org cooperation (#3), mutual support networks (#4), community governance (#5), conscious consumption (#10), women-led empowerment (#12), cultural preservation (#13), economy-as-healing (#14)**. None has a single entity, endpoint, or UI in any of the seven systems (verified Phase 4 + fresh greps). The platform behind the SSE landing is, mechanically, (a) a CR fiscal/POS system that works and (b) a storefront-builder whose commerce loop is broken (org-mode product endpoints missing, checkout persists nothing). There is no buyer identity at all — Cognito users are org operators — so every "community" principle lacks even its actor.

### Sequencing logic

1. **Stop contradicting the principles** (transparency fixes + secrets encryption + authorization mounting). A solidarity brand running on IDOR-open infrastructure with plaintext taxpayer certificates is the inverse of its promise; this is also the cheapest phase since most enforcement code exists unmounted.
2. **Make the implemented strength the story**: reposition the landing around what is genuinely solidaristic and real today — free CR-compliant invoicing, WhatsApp/offline-first selling for low-connectivity micro-sellers — and move barter/fairs copy to explicit roadmap framing.
3. **Build the community domain in dependency order**: public catalog fix → buyer identity → local discovery → fairs → barter/bundles/crowdfunding/governance. Each later mechanism depends on actors and surfaces created by the earlier ones.

## 5. Verdict

The Tsuru/Tsuru ecosystem today is a **fiscally excellent, socially empty** platform: of ~15 SSE principles claimed across landing and research, only *local fit* (CR fiscal/channel localization), *intra-org collaboration* (memberships/invitations), and *free access* (by absence of billing) have any mechanical embodiment — while the two most-repeated trust values (*transparencia*, *comercio justo*) are actively contradicted by fake flows and missing authorization. Every community-shaped differentiator (trueque, ferias, redes de apoyo, governance, conscious consumption, cultural preservation) is present-tense marketing with zero code behind it, and lacks even the buyer/community-member actor needed to build it. The good news is asymmetric: the contradiction fixes are mostly wiring of already-written code, the i18n/copy mechanisms are trivial, and the platform's truly differentiated asset — free legal e-invoicing for the smallest sellers — is itself an unclaimed solidarity story.
